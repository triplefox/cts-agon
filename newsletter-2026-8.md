Agon Newsletter, July-August 2026

During July and August I have been busy showcasing the Agon platform at various events: California Extreme, Vintage Computer Festival West, and MagWest.
So I've gotten behind on actually posting this newsletter although I've tried to keep a log going.
For the rest of the year I'll be back to my First Friday showcases at MADE.

The [AED text editor](https://github.com/avalonbits/aed/releases/tag/v0.16.0) has been updated to version 0.16.

Cartooning the Screen and a-nib projects:

As part of showcasing I started to produce comic pages. The intro can be seen on the [CTS front page](https://triplefox.github.io/cts-agon/)
and the rest are under the "Cartooning the Screen" header on that page.

I implemented a breadboard version of the Console8 joypads, my first digital electronics project.
I also made a comic for it!

I created a launcher application to help demo the system. It can be seen in the current a-nib distro(it starts up from autoexec.txt)

The a-nib framework is entering its fourth iteration, with one more revision to the architecture to try to work through things revealed by the launcher project.
I still haven't made a game in it yet, but I'm getting closer.
